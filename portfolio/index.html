How it works

  1. Send what you have. A sketch, photos with a ruler or calipers in the shot, or an existing STL, STEP or PDF.
  2. I confirm the details. Critical dimensions, hole patterns, what it attaches to, and how you plan to make it.
  3. I model it. A clean parametric solid, with one round of revisions included.
  4. You get your files. STEP + STL, plus a short notes file listing which dimensions were confirmed, which were estimated, and any open questions.

What I don't take on

Enclosures and casings · seals and O-rings · latches and mechanisms · structural or residential drafting · cosmetic surfacing · 4-axis work · fixture design · production runs · load-bearing life-safety parts · anything that is itself a regulated firearm part.

If your job is one of these, I'll tell you straight away and point you somewhere better if I can.
