FAQ

How is this different from the $29 Forge Lens Playbook?

The $29 starter sets up one read-only Web3 agent on your own machine. The Master Playbook does not duplicate that guide. It teaches the orchestration layer: a Foreman, specialists, routines, local routing and verification, CAD over MCP, shop fulfillment, finance agents, Cursor shipping, dashboards, and backups.

What do I need?

A Linux home server with 8 GB RAM or more for always-on tasks, backups, and the task ledger; a workstation if you want a larger local model; a private mesh between machines so local ports stay off the public internet; and the AI and coding tools you already use.

Is this a hosted service?

No. Docs and templates only. You supply the computers, local models, subscriptions, and backup storage. No earnings claims, and no agent spends or sends without your yes.

What about CAD?

Commercial or sellable parts go through FreeCAD, or through a CAD license you have cleared for paid work. The kit includes a license-check rule. The Picatinny rail example, when it is mentioned in shop notes, was cut on a 3-axis machine.
