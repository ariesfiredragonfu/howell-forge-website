Forge Lens

Read-only Web3 agent kit

Forge Lens is a read-only Web3 agent template set for builders who want to study wallet-adjacent ops patterns on a local fork before they ship anything. It is not a hosted crew, not live trading software, and not quantum marketing.

Safety boundary: example agents are hard-coded to refuse moving funds—no signing or sending transactions as part of the kit positioning. You inspect prompts, roles, and wiring; you add keys and scope only when you deliberately extend the fork on your machine.

What you get

  • Read-only Web3 agent templates — study roles, prompts, and wiring without live keys baked in.
  • Local fork — clone, run, and experiment on your machine or your infra.
  • Hard-coded refusal to move funds — templates are positioned for observation and learning, not autonomous transfers.
  • ElizaOS foundation — same ecosystem family as Agent Forge, scoped for study rather than production ops.
