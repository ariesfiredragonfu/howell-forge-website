Part requests

Send me your part

Mounts, brackets, rails, plate adapters and clamps. Send a sketch, photos or a file and I'll tell you quickly whether it's a good fit.

What helps me quote fast

  • A sketch, or photos with a ruler or calipers in the shot
  • What the part attaches to, plus any critical dimensions and hole patterns
  • How you plan to make it: 3D print, 3-axis machining, or not sure yet
  • An existing STL, STEP or PDF drawing if you have one

What you get back

STEP + STL (native Fusion 360 file on request), and for CNC-ready jobs, setup notes and a dimensioned PDF. See packages.

Good to know

  • I model parts. I don't run production. Quantity just helps me design for how it'll be made.
  • I don't take on enclosures, seals, latches or mechanisms, structural drafting, 4-axis work, fixture design, or regulated firearm parts.
